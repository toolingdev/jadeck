Presets

Tap a preset to move the camera. Keyboard: 1–9 recall, arrows/WASD move, Q/E zoom.

Advanced settings

Image, white balance, exposure, focus and noise settings, straight from the camera.

Image

The basic look of the picture: brightness, color strength, contrast, sharpness and tint.

White balance

Matches the light in the room so whites look white instead of yellow or blue.

Tap something in the picture that should be pure white — a shirt, a wall, a sheet of paper. The camera will aim at it, set its white balance, and come right back. This is the white-balance method that works on this camera today; the camera does not offer the mode and gain fields above directly, so they stay here for reference.

Exposure

How much light the camera lets in. Auto handles it; the other modes are for tricky lighting.

Focus

How eagerly the autofocus reacts, and which part of the frame it pays attention to.

Noise reduction

Cleans up the grainy speckle you get in dim light. Too much can make motion look smeary.
